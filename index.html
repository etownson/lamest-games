Ultra Ultralights

Ultralight flying · racing · combat · free flight

Strap into a lawn chair with wings and take to the sky. Race friends through courses, chase a spot on the time-trial leaderboard, team up against AI in co-op combat, or settle things in a PvP dogfight. Or just fly around and look at stuff.

Neon Wars

Twin-stick shooter · inspired by Geometry Wars

A glowing arena, an ever-growing swarm of angry geometry, and you in a tiny ship with a big gun. Move with one stick, shoot with the other, and survive as long as you can. Then do it again, because your name isn't high enough on the leaderboard yet.

  • Twin-stick controls
  • Endless waves

Hyperbolic Holdings

Satirical animated explainer

A 2½-minute investor pitch for a company that doesn't exist. Three increasingly unhinged prompts to Claude take its profits from a sensible 7% to a true hyperbola that hits infinity by Q3. Claude gives sensible advice every time, and the founders stamp right over it.

From a single paragraph, Claude wrote the script, coded the animation, composed the score and recorded an AI narrator, then synced it all and rendered the video.

The Dev Who's responsible for all this.

Lamest Gamer has 30+ years of experience as an entrepreneurial software developer — building products, companies, and the occasional thing that actually shipped on time.

Lamest Games is what happens when that experience meets free time and modern AI coding tools. Every game here is built with AI as a development partner, then played, tuned, and polished until it's genuinely fun. It's a hobby, so the name keeps expectations low. The games are there to beat them.
